Is this a real festival?

No. Afterhours is an imagined experience designed by Avalon Creatives Group. There is no venue, ticket inventory or event booking.

How does the planner work?

Add sessions from the programme. Sessions on the same night cannot overlap. Back-to-back sessions are allowed in this demonstration; travel time is not modeled.

What happens to my selection?

Your plan is held in the current page only. No personal information is requested, stored or sent. Refresh or use Clear the Plan to start again.
